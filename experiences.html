Experiences

On the reserve · events · nearby

Game drives at first light, vows under a Marula tree, long nights at the boma.

Everything happens on or near our 5,000-hectare private reserve, less than two hours from Johannesburg and Pretoria. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll put it together.

01 · On the reserve

Game drives

More than 50 animal species live on the 5,000-hectare reserve, from giraffe and nyala to warthog and cheetah.

Head out with a guide in the early morning or late afternoon, when the bush is at its most active.

  • Guided
  • Morning & afternoon
  • 50+ species
